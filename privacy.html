Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

In short

  • Flote does not collect any data. We have no servers, accounts, analytics, ads or trackers.
  • Your notes, settings and trash are stored only on your Mac, inside the app's own sandboxed folder.
  • Private notes are encrypted on your Mac with a key kept in your Keychain. Nobody but you can open them.

Data we collect

None. Flote works entirely offline and never sends your notes or any personal information anywhere.

Purchases

Flote Pro is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple processes the payment and the Apple ID it uses; we never receive your name, email or payment details. Restoring purchases is handled by Apple.

Siri, Shortcuts and notifications

If you use Siri or Shortcuts with Flote, Apple processes your request under its own privacy policy. Reminder notifications are delivered locally by macOS.

Children

Flote is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, export or delete on our side. You can export your notes to Markdown from Flote, and uninstalling the app removes all its data from your Mac.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
