Support

Something not working, or have an idea? Write to soporte.flotemac@outlook.com. Tell us your Mac model, macOS version and what you were doing. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a note?

Press Control-Option-N from any app, click the Flote icon in the menu bar, or ask Siri "Create a note in Flote". Control-Option-V creates a note with whatever you copied.

How do I use a ghost note again?

Hold Option-Command: ghost notes wake up while you hold the keys and stay active while the pointer is over them.

I bought Flote Pro. How do I get it on another Mac?

Open Flote Settings and click "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID.

I deleted a note by mistake.

Deleted notes stay 30 days in the trash. Open the Flote menu bar icon, go to Trash and click the note to restore it.

I forgot how to open a private note.

Private notes open with Touch ID or your Mac login password. If the Mac asks whether Flote can use its Keychain key, choose "Always Allow".

Can I import my notes from Stickies?

Yes. In the Flote menu bar icon choose "Import from Stickies" and select the Stickies folder that Flote suggests.
