Flote

Sticky notes that float on top of every app. Flote keeps your notes in sight on every desktop without getting in the way: ghost mode, reminders that pop up on time, notes per app and private notes with Touch ID.

  • Ghost mode. The note fades and your clicks go to the app underneath.
  • Reminders. At the right time the note appears, bounces and lets you know.
  • Notes per app. A note can show up only when a specific app is in front.
  • Private notes. Locked with Touch ID or your Mac password and stored encrypted.
